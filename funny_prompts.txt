Funny LTX 2 Prompts - Deadpan Absurd Comedy Style
===================================================

Default LTX 2 Prompt:
----------------------
A warm sunny backyard. The camera starts in a tight cinematic close-up of a woman and a man in their 30s, facing each other with serious expressions. The woman, emotional and dramatic, says softly, "That's it... Dad's lost it. And we've lost Dad." The man exhales, slightly annoyed: "Stop being so dramatic, Jess." A beat. He glances aside, then mutters defensively, "He's just having fun." The camera slowly pans right, revealing the grandfather in the garden wearing enormous butterfly wings, waving his arms in the air like he's trying to take off. He shouts, "Wheeeew!" as he flaps his wings with full commitment. The woman covers her face, on the verge of tears. The tone is deadpan, absurd, and quietly tragic.


1. Courtroom Drama
-------------------
A courtroom drama. The camera starts in a tight cinematic close-up of a lawyer in a suit, speaking with gravitas: "Your honor, the evidence is clear and overwhelming." The camera slowly pulls back, revealing the defendant is a houseplant accused of disturbing the peace. The plant's lawyer presents a leaf as Exhibit A. The judge, a cat in a robe, bangs a tiny gavel. The tone is deadpan legal absurdity with quiet dignity.


2. Funeral Service
-------------------
A funeral service in a beautiful chapel. The camera starts in a tight close-up of a woman in black, tears streaming down her face. She whispers, "He was taken from us too soon." The camera slowly pans to reveal the casket contains a USB flash drive. Mourners place flowers on the USB drive. A priest gives a eulogy about the drive's 256GB capacity and legacy. The tone is absurd and quietly tragic.


3. Job Interview
-----------------
A job interview in a corporate office. The camera starts in a tight close-up of a professional interviewer, asking: "Tell me about your strengths." The candidate responds confidently: "I'm detail-oriented, punctual, and I have excellent communication skills." The camera slowly reveals the candidate is a fax machine. The interviewer nods approvingly and asks about salary expectations. The fax machine beeps in response. The tone is deadpan corporate reality.


4. Romantic Dinner
-------------------
A romantic dinner for two. The camera starts in a tight close-up of two wine glasses clinking, soft candlelight flickering. A voice says, "To us, my love." The camera slowly pulls back to reveal one person is dining with their reflection in a mirror. The reflection occasionally doesn't match their movements. The waiter (the actual reflection) takes their order. The tone is surreal romantic comedy with existential undertones.


5. Space Mission Control
-------------------------
A space mission control room. The camera starts in a tight close-up of a mission commander, speaking gravely: "We've lost contact with the Mars rover. This is a critical situation." The camera slowly reveals all the scientists are hamsters in tiny space suits, operating the control panels by running on wheels. One hamster solemnly types a message with tiny paws. The tone is deadpan scientific drama with adorable undertones.


6. Business Meeting
--------------------
A completely normal business meeting. The camera starts in a tight close-up of a CEO in a suit, presenting quarterly earnings. He speaks professionally: "As you can see, our profits are up 300%." The camera slowly zooms out, revealing he's presenting to a conference room full of rubber ducks. One duck raises its wing to ask a question. The tone is deadpan corporate absurdity.


7. Wedding Ceremony
--------------------
A wedding ceremony in a beautiful garden. The camera starts in a tight close-up of the bride, tears of joy streaming down her face. She says emotionally, "I've waited my whole life for this moment." The camera slowly pans to reveal she's marrying a potted plant. The plant has a bow tie. The groom (a human) stands off to the side, eating cake and shrugging. The tone is surreal and quietly ridiculous.


8. Therapy Session
-------------------
A therapy session in a cozy office. The camera starts in a tight close-up of a therapist taking notes, nodding sympathetically. The client says, "I feel like I'm not being heard." The camera slowly reveals the client is a refrigerator. The therapist responds, "Tell me more about your cooling mechanisms." The tone is absurd and therapeutic.


9. Graduation Ceremony
-----------------------
A graduation ceremony at a prestigious university. The camera starts in a tight close-up of a proud student in cap and gown, holding their diploma. They say, "Four years of hard work finally paid off." The camera slowly pans to show the diploma reads "Professional Cloud Watcher" and the student is a golden retriever. The audience (all humans) applaud enthusiastically. The tone is deadpan educational satire.


10. Cooking Show
-----------------
A cooking show on television. The camera starts in a tight close-up of a celebrity chef, speaking to the camera: "Today we're making the perfect soufflé." The camera slowly pulls back to reveal the chef is actually a squirrel, the kitchen is in a tree, and the audience (other squirrels) are taking notes. The soufflé is made of acorns. The tone is absurd culinary excellence.


11. Library Study Session
--------------------------
A quiet library study session. The camera starts in a tight close-up of a student, intensely focused on a textbook. They whisper, "I need to understand this concept completely." The camera slowly reveals they're studying "How to Be a Chair" and the student is actually a chair, trying to learn how to be a better chair. Other chairs are studying around them. The librarian (a human) shushes them. The tone is academic absurdity with determination.


12. Speed Dating Event
-----------------------
A speed dating event in a modern cafe. The camera starts in a tight close-up of two people having an intense conversation. One says, "What do you do for a living?" The other responds, "I'm a professional shadow." The camera slowly pulls back to reveal one person is dating their own shadow on the wall. The shadow gestures dramatically as it explains its career goals. The tone is romantic comedy with existential depth.


13. News Broadcast
-------------------
A breaking news broadcast. The camera starts in a tight close-up of a news anchor, speaking seriously: "We have breaking news of national importance." The camera slowly reveals the news is about a missing sock. A detective (also a sock) presents evidence. The other sock, found, gives an emotional interview about the separation. The tone is deadpan journalistic drama.


14. Yoga Class
---------------
A serene yoga class. The camera starts in a tight close-up of an instructor, speaking calmly: "Let's begin with deep breathing." The camera slowly pans to show the entire class is made of furniture - chairs, tables, lamps - all in downward dog position. A coffee table struggles with a particularly difficult pose. The instructor (a human) adjusts a lamp's alignment. The tone is peaceful absurdity.


15. Police Interrogation
-------------------------
A dark police interrogation room. The camera starts in a tight close-up of a detective, asking: "Where were you on the night of the crime?" The camera slowly reveals the suspect is a tomato, nervously sweating. The detective continues: "We have witnesses who saw you at the scene." The tomato responds by blushing redder. A lawyer tomato enters, requesting a salad. The tone is noir absurdity with vegetable crime drama.
